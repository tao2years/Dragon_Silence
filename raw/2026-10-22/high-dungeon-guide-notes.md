## Venomous Tomb

- High layers add a teamwide boss shield and orbs that gain damage as the boss attacks.
- Failure before the first cycle indicates insufficient tank durability: examples include Toin transferring stats, ice/electric shield supports, or Isolde for attack reduction plus shield.
- Failure between the first and third cycles indicates insufficient recovery.
- Failure after the third cycle indicates insufficient damage because surviving orbs scale up; high-end frost AoE or low-end Ice Old Man + Rava are suggested.
- Ancestral Guardian on a healer can share tank damage. Blue Oak suits naturally hard tanks; Snowfield Guardian improves uptime for skill-dependent tanks.

## Cursed Tomb

- High layers make the first skill add two damage buffs.
- The third skill removes one ally buff and deals 50% extra damage to allies with no buff.
- The team therefore needs multi-buff removal and adequate friendly-buff coverage before skill three.
- Historical 79-level layer-14 team: Garius + Isitarian + Copy Lady + Shook + Voresh. Shook and Voresh were built to about 250 accuracy. Manual sequence: first Garius cast after the shield, Shook when the shield appears, Voresh shortly after the boss ultimate.
- High-layer no-gold-support sample: Garius with Gatekeeper Staff + Shook + Glover + Nathaniel + Atar. Glover removes two buffs; Nathaniel provides regeneration plus defense-up. The two turn-push effects are unstable, so the author recommends manual play.

## Rot Tomb

- High layers add recharge-speed reduction; the boss also transfers its level-2 attack reduction to the team through skill two.
- The team needs debuff immunity or removal in addition to solving the swallow mechanic.
- One recommended pattern is Fearless damage + Princess cleanse/immunity + Ice Shield mitigation; burst is held for the swallowed-ally release window.
- Taya timing: cast before each boss skill two; swallow follows about 6 seconds later, so her 10-second immunity/stealth covers both skill two and skill three. Catherine acts before the second-cycle skill one. Taya, Catherine and the shield support need enough haste to follow the historical 18-second cycle.
- Taya's stealth only applies to ranged heroes; suggested damage examples were Electric Bow + Ancient Wood, with Storm Falcon / Atar substitutions.

- Historical pre-layer-16 Rot Tomb sample: Ice Dragon + Ice Shield + Taya + Light Healer + Ice Woman.
- Taya uses 10-second team stealth before the swallow lock-on; the healer places debuff immunity before skill one; Ice Woman supplies defense reduction/frost support.
- Low-rarity substitutions mentioned: Ice Old Man for Ice Dragon and Ice protagonist for shielding.

Third concrete sample: https://www.taptap.cn/moment/653962912133548920

- Historical 79-level layer-14 Venomous Tomb: Estella + Garius + Isitarian + Copy Lady + Shook. Shook manually removes the boss shield and applies level-2 attack down; author used about 250 accuracy.
- Historical 79-level layer-14 Rot Tomb: Estella + Garius + Isitarian + Copy Lady + Sigh King. The boss targeted middle, then one tile left, then one tile right; small undead was placed in the middle and dragons on the sides. Ultimate order was adjusted so Copy Lady did not copy the small undead.
