# S9 release and community model notes

Captured: 2026-10-22 Asia/Shanghai

This file is an immutable research note. It separates official storefront facts from community inference.

## Official storefront

Source: https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/%E9%BE%99%E6%81%AF-%E7%A5%9E%E5%AF%82/id6480042764?platform=ipad

- App Store version history lists version `0.340.190489` on 2026-09-28.
- The release text identifies S9 as `暗潮将至` and advertises a first-week `110抽` package of season rewards.
- This establishes the S9 version date. It does not establish that a new S1 account receives 110 usable orange dice immediately; the promotional total can mix reward types and time gates.

## Current community long-form model

Source: https://www.taptap.cn/moment/809517361991256805

- The S7-era author states that card-pool rhythm became synchronized across all servers from S7 onward.
- On 2026-05-29 the author predicted that the following season would reinforce Ice Blast, Thunderbolt and Burn. The later official S8 sequence was exactly Featured I Ice Blast, Featured II Thunderbolt and Featured III Burn. This is a useful one-cycle backtest, not proof that every future prediction is reliable.
- The author separates long fights (Continental Challenge, season boss, river/sea-god variants) from sub-minute modes, and recommends that S1 accounts prioritize a team that can handle long fights without being useless in short fights.
- At that date, the author rated modern Toxin/Poison as T1-near-T0 after Serpentemis fixed the Rosell/Sleight interaction problem, but still flagged weak one-support survivability and future power-creep risk.
- The same article rated Burn lower at that time, but still described Durim as the core non-dynamic damage dealer that strongly wants five inspirations and Calver as the defense-break role. Flora/Wild was described as an expensive full-team project rather than a cheap S1 solution.
- These rankings are dated community judgments. Only the structural observations and the independently backtested S8 prediction are retained.

## Heartwish timing and server-class caveat

Sources:

- https://www.taptap.cn/app/237749/topic?group_label_id=2075528&page=5
- https://www.douyin.com/video/7690459413777493482
- https://www.taptap.cn/forum/g335048?page=27

- A TapTap player reported seeing Heartwish after roughly ten days on a new server and received the `100 Oggok / 200 Twitch / 300 Serpentemis` recommendation.
- A creator answering a player who explicitly identified server 364 as a celebration server estimated about one month from a 2026-09-28 start before the Serpentemis-selectable pool.
- A separate TapTap reply characterized the 800-step Heartwish as a lagging-season catch-up benefit and said a celebration server may not have it.
- The samples may refer to different server classes and global event windows. They support a Day 10–30 watch window, but do not prove a fixed per-server unlock day.

## S9 Featured I unresolved

- Public sources establish S9 start on 2026-09-28, Featured II around 2026-10-14 and Featured III on 2026-10-22.
- A syndicated Serpentemis-era article about Sionis was published 2026-10-13, but this does not recover the exact Featured I opening date.
- A cadence inference places Featured I near 2026-10-05/06, but this remains an estimate and is not entered as an official date.
